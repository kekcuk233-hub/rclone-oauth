# Privacy Policy **Last updated:** October 23, 2026 ## 1. Introduction This application uses the Google Drive API through rclone to upload and manage personal backup archives. It is intended for personal use only. ## 2. Information Collection This application does not independently collect, sell, or share personal information. It accesses Google Drive files and related metadata only as authorized by the account owner. ## 3. Use of Google Drive Data Google Drive access is used exclusively to create, upload, overwrite, and verify personal backup archives. The application does not use Google Drive data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose. ## 4. Data Storage and Security Backup archives are stored in the user's local home directory and Google Drive. Archives are not encrypted by this application before upload. Users are responsible for protecting their devices, Google accounts, and backup contents. OAuth credentials and access tokens are used to authenticate with Google services. Users should keep these credentials confidential. ## 5. Data Sharing This application does not sell or share user data with third parties. Data is transmitted to Google Drive as required for the backup process and is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Backup archives are retained in the user's Google Drive until overwritten or deleted by the user or the configured backup process. Users can delete their archives and revoke the application's Google account access at any time through their Google Account settings. ## 7. Third-Party Services This application relies on Google Drive and the rclone software. Use of Google services is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 8. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. ## 9. Contact For privacy-related questions, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the Google account used to configure the application.
